# Data, Crawling and Outreach Compliance

## Purpose
Keep the system designed around authorized data sources and responsible outreach.

## Data Sources
- Prefer official APIs or authorized providers.
- Follow provider terms and usage restrictions.
- Do not bypass CAPTCHAs, authentication, paywalls or anti-bot protections.
- Respect applicable crawling restrictions.

## Business Data
Only collect information necessary for the intended workflow.

Keep source attribution where useful.

## Email Outreach
The system should support:
- manual approval before initial sending
- unsubscribe handling
- suppression list
- bounce handling
- clear sender identity
- appropriate rate limits

Applicable privacy, electronic-marketing and anti-spam requirements vary by country and audience. Verify the requirements for each target market before commercializing automated outreach.

## AI
AI-generated personalization must be evidence-based.

Do not fabricate:
- employee names
- business problems
- performance metrics
- customer complaints
- claims about the company

## Data Retention
Define retention periods before production.
Allow deletion of lead records and outreach data when appropriate.

## Audit Trail
Record:
- source
- discovery date
- audit date
- outreach approval
- send event
- unsubscribe/suppression event

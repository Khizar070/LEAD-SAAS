# Lead Scoring

## Purpose
Prioritize prospects for manual review.

The score must be transparent and explainable.

## Example Signals

Website:
- no website: +30
- unreachable website: +20
- poor mobile result: +15
- poor performance: +10
- important SEO issue: +10
- weak CTA/contact flow: +10
- no clear automation opportunity: 0
- strong AI automation opportunity: +10

Business fit:
- target niche: +10
- target location: +10

## Rules
- Cap score at 100.
- Keep raw signals so the score can be explained.
- Do not treat score as an objective quality judgment.
- Make weights configurable.
- Do not let AI silently alter the score.

## Opportunity Levels
- 0–30: low
- 31–60: medium
- 61–80: high
- 81–100: very high

These are internal prioritization labels.
